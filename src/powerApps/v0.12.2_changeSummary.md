## Executive summary

`v0.12.2` is a major go-live readiness step that moves the Canvas app beyond the `v0.9.26` baseline by introducing new operational screens (`ManageUsers`, `CreateMeeting`), retiring legacy screens (`alt_ManageDesks`, `Screen1`, `Screen2`), and expanding connected SharePoint list coverage (`MasterScheduleList`, `SuperUserList`, `backupList_DeskReservations`, `Learning Lab Sessions`). The release keeps the training-reservation data model intact while strengthening workflow orchestration and backup/fallback persistence paths in `Confirm`. Version-string alignment improved from a drifted state to a consistent `0.12.2` across `.msapp`, `App.OnStart` (`varRepoVersion`), and `CanvasManifest.AppDescription`. `BindingErrorCount` fell materially (`329 -> 120`), which is positive for pre-go-live quality posture, though remaining warnings should still be validated in App Checker before production cutover.

---

## Functional impact and rationale

## 1. Administration and governance capabilities expanded

### What changed

- Added `ManageUsers` screen (`Src/ManageUsers.fx.yaml`, +89,944 bytes).
- Added `CreateMeeting` screen (`Src/CreateMeeting.fx.yaml`, +43,527 bytes).
- Startup/menu logic now includes user-management and release/help navigation in broader role branches.

### Why it matters

This closes a practical operational gap from earlier versions where booking operations evolved faster than in-app administration surfaces. For a go-live week, having explicit user-management and meeting-creation pathways reduces dependency on out-of-band admin intervention.

---

## 2. Legacy UI paths retired; screen map simplified

### What changed

Removed screens:

- `alt_ManageDesks`
- `Screen1`
- `Screen2`

Added screens:

- `ManageUsers`
- `CreateMeeting`

Net screen count moved from `22 -> 21` in `CanvasManifest.ScreenOrder`.

### Why it matters

This reduces duplication and technical debt in navigation surface area. Removing `alt_ManageDesks` specifically indicates consolidation toward one active desk-management flow instead of maintaining dual paths.

---

## 3. Reservation submission reliability improved

### What changed

In `Confirm`:

- Main `Desk Reservations` patch flow retains detailed reservation fields.
- Explicit fallback patch paths are present.
- Backup persistence path to `backupList_DeskReservations` is implemented.
- Super-user event logging patches into `SuperUserList`.

Representative persisted fields include:

- `submitterEmail_text`
- `studentEmail_text`
- `reservationScenario_text`
- `reservationRole_text`
- `reservationStart_datetime`
- `reservationEnd_datetime`

### Why it matters

For an imminent go-live, this improves resilience against transient patch failures and supports auditability by writing to both primary and backup pathways. The added SuperUser tracking also supports governance/reporting use cases.

---

## 4. Broader SharePoint integration introduced

### What changed

New data-source bindings appear in both source metadata and table definitions:

- `MasterScheduleList`
- `SuperUserList`
- `backupList_DeskReservations`
- `Learning Lab Sessions`

### Why it matters

The app is no longer constrained to the narrower three-list model. This allows richer training orchestration (schedule references, supervision tracking, and backup persistence) without requiring immediate schema redesign of existing core lists.

---

## 5. Identity/version consistency improved

### What changed

- `App.OnStart`: `varRepoVersion` updated from `0.9.26` to `0.12.2`.
- `CanvasManifest.AppDescription`: updated from `v0.9.17` to `v0.12.2`.

### Why it matters

This resolves prior-cycle version drift and reduces release ambiguity during production support and validation.

---

## 6. Quality and diagnostics posture

### What changed

- `BindingErrorCount`: `329 -> 120`.

### Why it matters

This indicates measurable reduction in unresolved binding concerns versus the previous cycle. Remaining errors still require targeted App Checker review before final go-live promotion.

## Release-position summary

This release is appropriately treated as a substantial forward move toward production readiness rather than an incremental patch. It combines structural UI changes, broader data-source integration, resilience-oriented patch strategy, and version consistency improvements. The remaining go-live gate is no longer foundational capability; it is targeted validation (App Checker residue, environment wiring, and operational hardening) captured in `v0.12.2_knownIssues.md` and `v0.12.2_recommendations.md`.
