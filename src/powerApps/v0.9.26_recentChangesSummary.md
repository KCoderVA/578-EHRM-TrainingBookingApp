# Canvas App Change Summary — v0.8.14 → v0.9.26

**Component:** `578-EHRM-Training-App` (Power Apps Canvas App)
**Compared versions:** archived **v0.8.14** → current **v0.9.26**
**Old source:** `archive/src/powerApps/v0.8.x/v0.8.14/.unpacked/`
**New source:** `src/powerApps/.unpacked/`
**Analysis date:** 2026-08-04
**Method:** File-by-file MD5-hash/size diff of the unpacked `.msapp` sources (`*.fx.yaml`, `CanvasManifest.json`, `DataSources/`, `pkgs/TableDefinitions/`, `Connections/`, `Components/`, `Assets/`), followed by targeted content inspection of every changed screen and every changed data-source schema.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Where **v0.8.14** was about *access* (an app-wide RBAC engine, impersonation, and a first-draft training Class/Session Picker), **v0.9.26** is about **data** — it turns the app from "book a room + pick a class" into a **complete training-registration record** that is actually persisted, attributable, and ready for a downstream approval/reminder workflow. No new screens were added this cycle; instead the existing booking pipeline (POCSUPERVISOR → Confirm) was deepened and the SharePoint back-end was substantially re-shaped. The headline changes:

- **The `Desk Reservations` SharePoint list schema was massively expanded** — roughly **40 new columns** were added, grouped into **submitter**, **student/attendee**, **reservation (scenario/role/session/location)**, **trainer approval**, and **automated-reminder** families. This is the single largest change of the release and the backbone of everything else.
- **The Class/Session Picker is now actually persisted.** The v0.8.14 follow-up gap (§9 item 1 of the prior summary — *"picker values are displayed but not written to SharePoint"*) is **closed**: the **Confirm** SUBMIT `Patch` now writes the full scenario/role/session/location plus separate submitter- and student-identity fields into the new columns.
- **POCSUPERVISOR grew `+63 KB`** — the Class/Session Picker was reworked with **semantic control names** (`dropdown_ScenarioPicker`, `dropdown_RolePicker`, `dropdown_DateTimePicker`, `dropdown_LocationPicker`), an **attendee people-picker** (`var_Attendees`) that formally enables *booking on behalf of other people*, a live HTML selection summary, a scenario-materials deep-link, and an admin `RESET` button.
- **Confirm grew `+11 KB`** — new review labels for the picker selections, a `varRecurrenceID` generator (`Max(existing)+1`), and the expanded persistence `Patch`.
- **Reservation Details grew `+6 KB`** — it now surfaces the new fields (`reservationScenario_text`, `reservationRole_text`, `reservationStart_datetime`, `submitterName_text`) and adds a *"Contact the EHRM Trainers about this item (#ID)"* action.
- **The `Desks` list schema was normalized/cleaned** — a batch of legacy, untyped, duplicate columns was removed in favor of the typed `*_choice` / `*_text` / `*_boolean` columns; `DeskSelect` was re-bound to the cleaned schema.
- **Two version-string fixes / one new drift.** `App.OnStart` now stamps `varRepoVersion = "0.9.26"` (was `"0.8.12"`), and the `varIsImpersonating` detection logic was corrected. However, the manifest `AppDescription` still lags at **"v0.9.17"** while the `.msapp` and `varRepoVersion` say **0.9.26** — a new, smaller drift.
- **No new screens, no new components, no image changes.** Two new modern control templates (`modernText`, `modernTextInput`) were registered and used in the reworked forms.

> **Cross-component version note.** The project-wide `VERSION` file is **0.8.17**, but this Canvas app component has moved onto its own faster line at **0.9.26** (per the repo's hybrid versioning policy). The v0.8.14 note about "bringing the Canvas app onto the project-wide SemVer line" did **not** hold — the component is now *ahead* of the project release number.

## 1A. Functional Impact & Design Rationale — the "Why" Behind the Changes

This section explains *what each change accomplishes for the end user* and *why it was made*. The v0.8.14 → v0.9.26 evolution is driven by one central idea — **make every booking a real, complete, attributable training record** — plus the supporting work that makes that idea usable.

### Theme 1 — The reservation becomes a real training record (the SharePoint schema expansion)

**What it is.** The `Desk Reservations` list gained ~40 purpose-built columns. Instead of a booking being little more than *"a desk/room, a time window, and who reserved it"*, a booking now captures the **entire who/what/when/where of a training session**, split into deliberate families:

| Column family | Examples | What it records |
|---|---|---|
| **Submitter** | `submitterName_text`, `submitterEmail_text`, `submitterService_text` | The person who *created* the request (may differ from the trainee — see Theme 2). |
| **Student / attendee** | `studentName_text`, `studentEmail_text`, `studentService_text`, `studentTitle_text`, `studentManagerEmail_text` | The person the training is *for*, plus their title/service and their manager's email (for notifications/approvals). |
| **Reservation detail** | `reservationScenario_text`, `reservationRole_text`, `reservationStart_datetime`, `reservationEnd_datetime`, `reservationRoom_text`, `reservationFloor_text`, `reservationBuilding_text`, `reservationDivision_text`, `reservationDesk_text` | The specific EHRM Learning-Lab scenario, the role being practiced, the session start/end, and the fully-decomposed location. |
| **Bodies / narrative** | `reservationAgreementBody_multilinetext`, `reservationDescription_multilinetext`, `reservationComments_multilinetext`, `reservationInviteBody_multilinetext`, `reservationInviteOptionalAttendees_multilinetext`, `reservationJustification_multilinetext` | The room agreement text, human-readable description, free-text comments, and the calendar-invite body / optional-attendee list. |
| **Trainer approval & recording** | `trainerApproved_datetime`/`_Email_text`/`_Comments_multilinetext`, `trainerReceived_datetime`/`_Email_text`/`_Comments_multilinetext`, `trainerRecordedInExcel_datetime`/`_text`/`Target_text` | A three-stage trainer lifecycle: *received → approved → recorded in the master Excel roster*. |
| **Automated reminders** | `reminderStudent1wPreClass_text`, `reminderStudent72hPreClass_text`, `reminderStudent1hPreClass_text`, `reminderStudent24hPostCreate_text`, and the matching `reminderTrainer*` set | Timestamp/flag columns a scheduler (Power Automate) can stamp as each reminder is sent (1 week / 72 h / 1 h before class, plus 24 h after creation), for both student and trainer. |

**Removed (legacy):** `DeskFloor`, `Floor`, and `Reason for desk reservation` — superseded by the new decomposed location fields and the narrative body columns.

**Why it was added.** In v0.8.14 the picker *collected* scenario/role/session/location but threw them away at submit time (nothing was written to SharePoint). That made attendance reporting, approvals, and reminders impossible. v0.9.26 gives every one of those data points a **typed home in the list**, so a single reservation row is now a self-contained training record that downstream automation and Power BI can consume. The **trainer** and **reminder** families in particular exist to be **populated by companion Power Automate flows**, not by the canvas app — they are the schema contract for an approval + notification pipeline (see §9).

### Theme 2 — Booking *on behalf of* someone else becomes a first-class flow

**What it is.** POCSUPERVISOR now has an explicit **attendee people-picker** whose selection lands in `var_Attendees`. At submit, the app records **two distinct identities**: the **submitter** (resolved live from `Office365Users.MyProfileV2()` — always the real signed-in person) and the **student** (resolved from `Office365Users.UserProfileV2(var_Attendees)` / `ManagerV2(var_Attendees)` — the chosen attendee).

**Why it was added.** v0.8.14 introduced *impersonation* as an admin support tool, but a booking still only had one "Reserved By" identity, so you could not cleanly express *"Coordinator X registered Trainee Y."* By persisting submitter and student separately, the app now supports the real-world case where a coordinator/supervisor books training for a staff member — and the trainee's manager email is captured for the approval/notification chain. The generated `Title` reflects this too: `<attendee> – <category> - <scenario> (<role>)`.

### Theme 3 — Closing the persistence gap (Confirm SUBMIT rewrite)

**What it is.** The **Confirm** screen's SUBMIT `Patch` (inside `ForAll(CollectionRecurrence) → ForAll(colSelectedDesks)`) was rewritten. It now maps the picker/identity variables into the new columns — `submitter*`, `student*`, `reservationScenario_text`, `reservationRole_text`, `reservationStart_datetime`, `reservationEnd_datetime`, `reservation{Room,Floor,Building,Division}_text`, `reservationAgreementBody_multilinetext`, `reservationInviteBody_multilinetext`, etc. — alongside the retained legacy fields (`Check Out From/To`, `Service`, `RoomReservation`, recurrence fields). `OutlookEventID`/`OutlookSeriesID`/`ParentReservationID` are patched as `Blank()`, i.e. **left for a flow to fill** once the calendar invite is created.

**Why it matters.** This is the change that makes Theme 1 real. It also confirms the intended architecture: the **canvas app owns capture**, while **Outlook invite creation, trainer approval, and reminders are delegated to Power Automate** (hence the deliberately-blank Outlook IDs and the empty trainer/reminder columns at insert time).

### Theme 4 — Data hygiene on the `Desks` inventory

**What it is.** The `Desks` list lost a pile of legacy/duplicate columns (`Active`, `Capacity`, `Description`, `Division`(+variants), `Floor`, `POC`, `SecondPOC`, `SmartBoard`, `VTel`, `Projector/TV`, `RoomLocation`, `MapLink`, `Asset`, `DeskLocation`, attachments) and standardized on typed columns (`DivisionName_choice`, `RoomName_choice`, `*_boolean`, `*_text`, etc.); `Title` is now surfaced as `ItemID_title`.

**Why it was made.** The room/desk inventory that feeds `DeskSelect` and the reservation flow had accumulated redundant "classic vs typed" pairs. Consolidating to one typed set reduces binding ambiguity and makes the location data that now flows into `reservation*_text` consistent. `DeskSelect`'s `+2.7 KB` change is the re-binding to this cleaned schema.

### Theme 5 — Version-stamp correctness & impersonation-flag fix

- `App.OnStart` now sets `varRepoVersion = "0.9.26"` (was `"0.8.12"`), so the Dashboard version badge finally reflects the real build.
- `varIsImpersonating` was **corrected**. The v0.8.14 expression returned `true` when `varRealEmail = varTargetEmail` (i.e. it flagged *not*-impersonating as impersonating); v0.9.26 uses the correct `varRealEmail <> varTargetEmail`. The Dashboard combobox handler mirrors this (`Set(varIsImpersonating, varTargetEmail <> varRealEmail)`).

## 3. App-Level Changes

### 3.1 `App.OnStart` — two targeted fixes (structure otherwise identical)

The six-section startup routine introduced in v0.8.14 (initialization/identity → find record → auto-provision → resolve level → admin flag → build menu) is **structurally unchanged**. Two edits:

1. **Version stamp corrected** — `Set(varRepoVersion, "0.8.12")` → **`Set(varRepoVersion, "0.9.26")`**. This is what the Dashboard version badge (`"v" & varRepoVersion`) renders, so the in-app version is now accurate.
2. **Impersonation detection fixed** — the `varIsImpersonating` expression changed from the inverted v0.8.14 logic:

   ```powerfx
   // v0.8.14 (buggy — true when real == target)
   If(varTargetEmail = "kyle.coder@va.gov", false,
      varTargetEmail <> "kyle.coder@va.gov" && varRealEmail = varTargetEmail, true,
      false)
   ```

   to the correct:

   ```powerfx
   // v0.9.26 (true only when acting as someone else)
   If(varTargetEmail = "kyle.coder@va.gov", false,
      varRealEmail <> varTargetEmail, true,
      false)
   ```

`StartScreen` remains `Dashboard`; the auto-provisioning `Patch` (default `"User"` level, manager lookup, guarded to the real user) is unchanged.

## 4. Screen-by-Screen Deep Dive

### 4.1 POCSUPERVISOR (`POCSUPERVISOR.fx.yaml`) — largest change (`+63 KB`)

The "create a booking" screen was the focus of the release. The v0.8.14 Class/Session Picker (`ctn_PopUp_ClassPicker` with generically-named `Dropdown3` / `3_1` / `3_2` / `3_3`) was reworked into a clearer, data-capturing flow:

- **Semantic control names.** The four picker dropdowns are now `dropdown_ScenarioPicker`, `dropdown_RolePicker`, `dropdown_DateTimePicker`, `dropdown_LocationPicker` (inside `ctn_PopUp_ClassPicker_Scenario` / `_Role` / `_DateTime` / `_Location`).
- **Attendee people-picker → `var_Attendees`.** A combobox people-picker (`DataCardValue3`) sets `var_Attendees` via `Concat(DataCardValue3.SelectedItems, Email, ";")` (or `Self.Selected.Email` for a single pick). This is the mechanism that lets a submitter register **someone else** for training.
- **Scenario-materials deep-link.** Selecting a scenario builds `var_scenarioTrainingMaterialsURL` (using `varPopUp_ClassPicker_scenarioSplit`, the space-encoded scenario name) and renders a *"Click here to open the &lt;SCENARIO&gt; training scenario materials…"* HTML link into the SharePoint Learning-Lab Workbook library.
- **Live selection summary.** `ctn_PopUp_ClassPicker_Header` renders an HTML recap (Scenario / Role / Date / Time / Location) that updates as choices are made.
- **Date/time parsing retained & centralized.** `Match`/`MatchAll` regex parses the selected session string into `varPopUp_ClassPicker_scenarioDate`, `…StartTime`, `…EndTime`, `…Start24`, `…End24`, and `…DayOfWeek`.
- **`Button1` (`modernButton`) RESET** clears `var_Attendees`, `var_reservationScenario`, and every `varPopUp_ClassPicker_*` variable in one press.
- **`OnVisible` state machine** still gates the popup: when `varPopUp_ClassPicker = 0` it `Concurrent`-clears all picker state; when `= 1` it re-hydrates the variables from the current dropdown selections.

**Net effect:** the picker no longer just *shows* choices — it now produces the clean set of variables (`var_reservationScenario`, `var_reservationRole`, `var_Attendees`, session times, decomposed location) that Confirm persists.

### 4.2 Confirm (`Confirm.fx.yaml`) — persistence rewrite (`+11 KB`)
