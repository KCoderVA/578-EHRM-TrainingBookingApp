## Risk summary for imminent go-live

1. The biggest operational risk is no longer missing feature capability; it is environment consistency and integration hardening.
2. Two high-severity items should be explicitly signed off pre-go-live: `KI-122-001` and `KI-122-007`.
3. Security posture is functionally acceptable for controlled deployment but should not be treated as final hardening complete while `KI-122-004` and `KI-122-005` remain open.
