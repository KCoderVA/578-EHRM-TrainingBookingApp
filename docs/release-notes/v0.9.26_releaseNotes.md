# EHRM Training & Booking App (Station 578) — Release Notes (v0.9.26)

- **Release date**: 2026-08-04
- **Release type**: Feature — turns every booking into a complete, persisted training record and expands the SharePoint back-end to support a trainer-approval + automated-reminder workflow.
- **Previous release**: v0.8.16 (2026-07-28) *(the interim `0.8.17` version bump was never released; it is superseded by this `0.9.26` release)*
- **Canvas app component**: v0.8.14 → **v0.9.26**
- **Power Automate**: `AppUserList` v0.1.0 *(unchanged)*

---

## Executive Summary

Where **v0.8.14** was about *access* (an app-wide RBAC engine, impersonation, and a first-draft Class/Session Picker), **v0.9.26 is about *data***. Every booking is now saved as a complete, attributable **training registration record**: the scenario/role/session/location the user picks is finally persisted to SharePoint, the person who *submitted* the request is recorded separately from the *student* the training is for, and the `Desk Reservations` list gained the trainer-approval and automated-reminder columns needed to drive a downstream Power Automate workflow. The project-wide version jumps `0.8.16 → 0.9.26` to re-align the repo release line with the Canvas app.

**5** distinct improvement areas are documented in full below:

1. **`Desk Reservations` schema expansion (~40 columns)** — the backbone of the release; a booking is now a full training record.
2. **Class/Session Picker persistence** — the picker values are now written in the Confirm SUBMIT `Patch` (closing the v0.8.14 gap).
3. **Booking on behalf of others** — a new attendee people-picker records submitter vs. student as distinct identities.
4. **POCSUPERVISOR rework** — semantic control names, scenario-materials deep-link, live selection summary, admin RESET.
5. **`Desks` list normalization + version/impersonation fixes** — typed columns, `varRepoVersion → 0.9.26`, corrected `varIsImpersonating`.

## Changes

### SharePoint — `Desk Reservations` schema expansion (data source)

Before v0.9.26 a booking recorded little more than a desk/room, a time window, and "Reserved By." The Class/Session Picker added in v0.8.14 *collected* scenario/role/session/location but discarded it at submit time, so attendance reporting, approvals, and reminders were impossible.

**What changed:** the `Desk Reservations` list gained roughly **40 purpose-built columns**, grouped into families:

| Column family | Examples | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Submitter** | `submitterName_text`, `submitterEmail_text`, `submitterService_text` | Who *created* the request. |
| **Student / attendee** | `studentName_text`, `studentEmail_text`, `studentService_text`, `studentTitle_text`, `studentManagerEmail_text` | Who the training is *for*, incl. their manager (for approvals/notifications). |
| **Reservation detail** | `reservationScenario_text`, `reservationRole_text`, `reservationStart_datetime`, `reservationEnd_datetime`, `reservationRoom_text`, `reservationFloor_text`, `reservationBuilding_text`, `reservationDivision_text`, `reservationDesk_text` | The specific EHRM Learning-Lab scenario, role, session, and decomposed location. |
| **Narrative bodies** | `reservationAgreementBody_multilinetext`, `reservationDescription_multilinetext`, `reservationComments_multilinetext`, `reservationInviteBody_multilinetext`, `reservationInviteOptionalAttendees_multilinetext`, `reservationJustification_multilinetext` | Agreement text, description, comments, and calendar-invite body. |
| **Trainer approval** | `trainerReceived_*`, `trainerApproved_*`, `trainerRecordedInExcel_*` (datetime + email + comments) | A received → approved → recorded-in-Excel lifecycle. |
| **Automated reminders** | `reminderStudent{1w,72h,1h}PreClass_text`, `reminderStudent24hPostCreate_text`, and matching `reminderTrainer*` | Timestamp/flag columns a scheduler stamps as each reminder is sent. |

**Removed:** `DeskFloor`, `Floor`, `Reason for desk reservation` (superseded by the decomposed-location and narrative-body columns).

> The **submitter / student / reservation / narrative** families are written by the app. The **trainer** and **reminder** families are the schema contract for companion **Power Automate** flows (see Upgrade Notes).

---

### Power Apps — Class/Session Picker persistence (Confirm.fx.yaml)

Previously the `varPopUp_ClassPicker_*` values were echoed on the Confirm screen but never saved.

**What changed:** the Confirm **SUBMIT** `Patch` (inside `ForAll(CollectionRecurrence) → ForAll(colSelectedDesks)`) now maps every captured value into the new columns — submitter identity (from `Office365Users.MyProfileV2()`), student identity (from `UserProfileV2`/`ManagerV2` of the chosen attendee), scenario/role, `reservationStart_datetime`/`reservationEnd_datetime`, the decomposed location, and the invite/agreement bodies — plus a structured `Title` (`<attendee> – <category> - <scenario> (<role>)`). `OutlookEventID`/`OutlookSeriesID`/`ParentReservationID` are deliberately inserted blank for a flow to back-fill.

---

### Power Apps — Booking on behalf of others (POCSUPERVISOR.fx.yaml)

v0.8.14 introduced admin impersonation as a support tool, but a booking still had only one identity.

**What changed:** POCSUPERVISOR gained an **attendee people-picker** (`var_Attendees`). The app now records the **submitter** (the real signed-in user) and the **student** (the chosen attendee, incl. their manager email) as distinct fields — cleanly expressing *"Coordinator X registered Trainee Y."*

---

### Power Apps — POCSUPERVISOR rework (POCSUPERVISOR.fx.yaml, `+63 KB`)

The Class/Session Picker was reworked for clarity and data capture:

- Semantic control names: `dropdown_ScenarioPicker`, `dropdown_RolePicker`, `dropdown_DateTimePicker`, `dropdown_LocationPicker` (was `Dropdown3` / `3_1` / `3_2` / `3_3`).
- A live HTML **selection summary** (Scenario / Role / Date / Time / Location).
- A **scenario-materials deep-link** into the SharePoint Learning-Lab Workbook library.
- An admin `RESET` button that clears all picker state in one press.

## Upgrade Notes / Configuration Requirements

> **Admin actions required before/after deploying this release:**
>
> 1. **Confirm the SharePoint `Desk Reservations` list has the ~40 new columns** (submitter / student / reservation / trainer / reminder families) with matching internal names, or the SUBMIT `Patch` will error. The 3 removed columns (`DeskFloor`, `Floor`, `Reason for desk reservation`) may be retired.
> 2. **Build/deploy the companion Power Automate flows** that populate the `trainer*` and `reminder*` columns and create the Outlook invite (back-filling `OutlookEventID`/`OutlookSeriesID`). The app inserts these blank.
> 3. **Populate `DeskAccessControl.AccessLevel_Text`** for all intended users and decide the unknown-user default (currently `User`, ideally `AccessDenied`).
> 4. **Run the Power Apps App Checker** and review the elevated `BindingErrorCount` (see Known Issues) before production use.

## Known Issues

- **`BindingErrorCount` rose 49 → 329.** Likely references to the removed `Desk Reservations`/`Desks` columns plus the modern Text/TextInput control migration. Review in the editor before the next production deployment.
- **Trainer/reminder columns are written blank** by the app — they depend on the not-yet-built Power Automate flows.
- **Residual version drift:** the manifest `AppDescription` reads `v0.9.17` while the `.msapp`/`varRepoVersion` read `0.9.26`.
- **Unknown-user RBAC default** is still `User` rather than `AccessDenied` (carried forward).
