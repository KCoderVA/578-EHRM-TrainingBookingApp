578 EHRM Training App

A ready-to-import Microsoft Power Platform solution that lets VA staff self-register (or be registered by a supervisor) for EHRM "Learning Lab" training, books the sessions on Outlook calendars, automates reminders and backups, and feeds leadership reporting — built and in production at Edward Hines Jr. VA Hospital (Station 578, VISN 12).

Executive At-a-Glance

Leadership

The Electronic Health Record Modernization (EHRM) transition requires every VA facility to train large numbers of clinical and administrative staff on the new Federal EHR — on a tight schedule, in limited Learning Lab seats, with supervisors coordinating on behalf of their teams. Hines VA replaced ad-hoc email, spreadsheet, and sign-up-sheet scheduling with this single, secure, mobile-friendly app that runs entirely inside the VA's existing Microsoft 365 / Power Platform (GCC High) tenant — no new licenses, servers, or contracts required.

The Problem It Solves & the Value Delivered

Leadership

Before: manual, fragmented scheduling

  • Training sign-ups tracked in emails, spreadsheets, and paper lists.
  • Supervisors could not easily register staff on their behalf.
  • No single view of who is booked, where, and when.
  • Completion data in TMS was disconnected from scheduling.

After: one self-service app

  • Staff book themselves from phone or desktop in a few taps.
  • Supervisors/POCs register students in a guided flow.
  • Outlook calendar invites and confirmation emails sent automatically.
  • Leadership sees bookings and TMS completions in Power BI.

Benefits for your facility

💲 Zero new cost

Uses only standard Microsoft 365 connectors (SharePoint, Outlook, Office 365 Users) already licensed for VA staff. No premium connectors, databases, or vendor contracts.

🔐 Secure by design

Runs inside the VA tenant under each user's own identity. 8 access tiers control who can view, book, manage rooms, or manage users. No PHI is stored.

⏱️ Saves coordinator time

Automatic invites, reminders, new-user onboarding, and backup records replace manual email chasing and spreadsheet reconciliation.

📊 Leadership visibility

Embedded Power BI dashboards and an automated TMS completion feed show training progress toward go-live readiness.

📱 Mobile-first

Responsive Canvas app works on phones, tablets, and desktops — staff can book from the floor.

🔁 Proven & portable

24 documented releases, full source control, and a step-by-step import guide make it straightforward to duplicate at another VA facility.

Solution Architecture

Developers

A Power Apps Canvas app is the user-facing front end. It reads/writes SharePoint Online lists on two sites, uses Office 365 Users and Office 365 Outlook connectors, and embeds Power BI (GCC). Four Power Automate cloud flows run independently on SharePoint/Outlook triggers — the app never calls a flow directly; they are linked by data and email.

Canvas App20 screens · Power Fx · RBAC · recurrence engine
SharePoint (app site)6 read/write lists · 578_EHRM_TrainingApp
SharePoint (national)7 read-only EHRM Sandbox lists/library
M365 ConnectorsOffice 365 Users · Office 365 Outlook
Power AutomateAppUserList · SendReminders · CreateBackups · parseTMSReports
Power BI (Gov)Signup Tool · SuperUser Dashboard · TMS Training Details
Canvas app (Confirm screen)
   │  Patch → Desk Reservations ─────────────► SendReminders flow ──► email + Teams card
   │  Office365Outlook.V4CalendarPostItem ───► attendee calendar invite
   │  SendEmailV2 "[REGISTERED] EHRM Learning Lab Training - …"
   ▼
CreateBackups flow (Outlook trigger) ──► backupList_DeskReservations

Admin edits DeskAccessControl ──► AppUserList flow ──► M365 profile enrichment + admin adaptive cards/email

TMS report email (~every 6 h) ──► parseTMSReportsToSharePoint flow
   ──► TMS_Reports/TMSProgramCompletion_DetailedReport.csv (+ dated archive) ──► Power BI refresh

Deployment QuickStart — Import Into Your Facility

Developers

Prerequisites

  • Power Platform environment in the US Gov (GCC High) cloud with Power Apps + Power Automate.
  • A SharePoint Online site you own for the app lists (and read access to the national EHRM Sandbox site, if reusing reference data).
  • Connections for SharePoint, Office 365 Users, Office 365 Outlook (and Teams for flow cards).
  • Optional: VS Code + Power Platform CLI (pac) for source-controlled ALM.

Steps

  1. Get the source — clone or download the public repository.
  2. Create the SharePoint lists — Desk Reservations, DeskAccessControl, Desks, MasterScheduleList (including the _text scheduling columns and scenarioDescription_multitext), SuperUserList, backupList_DeskReservations.
  3. Import the Canvas app — Power Apps → Apps → Import canvas app → src/powerApps/.msapp/v1.3.5_578EHRMTrainingApp.msapp.
  4. Re-point data sources — replace every dvagov.sharepoint.com/sites/… URL, list GUID, and tenant/environment GUID with your own; re-point the Screen1 Power BI embed.
  5. Seed access — add yourself to DeskAccessControl with AccessLevel_Text = "AppAdmin", then manage others from the in-app Users screen.
  6. Import the flows — import each legacy package, map connections, update site/list references, sender filters, and notification recipients; turn on.
  7. Optional analytics — open src/analytics/powerBI/.pbit/Signup Tool.pbit, point to your lists, publish to your Gov workspace, and share with app users.
  8. Sanitize & test — remove Hines-specific emails/comments, run a test booking end-to-end (record, invite, email, reminder, backup), then publish.

Source-control loop (pac CLI)

# Unpack a newly exported .msapp into reviewable source
pac canvas unpack --msapp dist/release/578EHRMTrainingApp.msapp --sources src/powerApps/.unpacked --force

# Re-pack source into an importable .msapp
pac canvas pack --msapp dist/release/578EHRMTrainingApp.msapp --sources src/powerApps/.unpacked/layoutDefault

# Export and unpack the solution
pac solution export --name "EHRMTrainingBooking" --path dist/release/EHRMTrainingBooking_Solution.zip --managed false
pac solution unpack --zipfile dist/release/EHRMTrainingBooking_Solution.zip --folder config/solutions/EHRMTrainingBooking --overwrite true
⚠️ Environment-specific values: unpacked source contains Hines site URLs, tenant/environment GUIDs, Power BI embed IDs, and some VA email addresses in comments. These require VA authentication and are not secrets, but must be replaced for your facility.

Security, Privacy & Compliance Snapshot

Developers
  • Identity: all access via the user's own Entra ID sign-in; no service accounts in the app; impersonation backdoor removed in v1.0.12.
  • Data: training scheduling and staff directory data only — no PHI/PII beyond VA work profile fields.
  • Least privilege: 8-tier RBAC; national reference lists are read-only.
  • Repo hygiene: data files (.csv/.xlsx), exports (.zip), and any local/ folder are git-ignored; PR security scan workflow.
  • Licensing: Apache 2.0; every source file carries the license header.
  • See SECURITY.md for reporting and sanitization guidance.
